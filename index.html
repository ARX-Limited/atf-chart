SOFR, EURIBOR and the spread

SOFR (USD) and SARON (CHF) are compounded in arrears over the selected calendar tenor, annualised on ACT/360. Both use the same accrual dates. If the start falls on a non-business day, it rolls back to the preceding joint US–Swiss benchmark date. EURIBOR (EUR) is the forward-looking fixing for that tenor on the observation date. These series compare different currencies and fixing conventions.
Monthly points average the daily observations available for all three benchmarks. Spread = matched-tenor SOFR minus EURIBOR, in percentage points.
Sources: New York Fed SOFR Index · Bank of Finland EURIBOR · SNB / SIX SARON · recent SARON fixings.
